youapp

Internal publishing tool for our own YouTube channels

youapp is a private tool used by a small team to manage the YouTube channels it owns. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that we produced to our own channels as private or scheduled videos.
  • Sets thumbnails and captions for those videos.
  • Reads channel and video statistics (YouTube Analytics) to plan future videos.
  • Reads public comments on our videos to understand audience feedback.

The tool only works with channels whose owners have signed in and granted access themselves through Google's consent screen.
