Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how youapp ("the app") handles data accessed through Google and YouTube APIs.

Who uses the app

The app is an internal tool. It is used only by its owners to manage YouTube channels that they own. It does not collect data from the general public.

Data we access

  • Basic information about the signed-in YouTube channel (channel ID, title).
  • Uploading videos, thumbnails and captions to that channel.
  • YouTube Analytics reports and statistics for that channel and its videos.
  • Public comments posted on that channel's videos.

How we use the data

The data is used only to publish our own videos and to understand how they perform. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens and reports are stored on a private server controlled by the owners. Access to the server is restricted.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

A channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, stored tokens and data for that channel are deleted within 30 days, or sooner on request.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email shown on the app's Google consent screen.
